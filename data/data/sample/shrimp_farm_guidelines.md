# Shrimp Farm Water Quality Guidelines

Farmers should monitor temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH, salinity, ammonia and turbidity.
Dissolved oxygen can be lower in the early morning after overnight respiration, so morning
monitoring is important. Aeration can be increased when oxygen is low.

Salinity changes should be managed gradually. Rapid dilution after heavy rainfall can create
salinity stress. Water-quality observations should be considered together with mortality,
feeding behavior, farm records and laboratory diagnostics rather than as a single disease test.
